## Who we are

TV Music Store ("we", "us") is a trading name of a UK general partnership of
Stanislav Barantsov and Maryna Huz. We are the **data controller** for personal
data processed through tvmusicstore.com. Contact: contact@tvmusicstore.com,
[[correspondence address]].

## What we collect and why

We keep data collection to the minimum needed to run the store.

| Data | When | Why (lawful basis) |
|------|------|--------------------|
| **Email, name** | Sign-up / Google sign-in | Create your account, log you in, send login codes and license emails — *performance of contract* |
| **Login codes / session** | Sign-in | Authenticate you securely — *contract* |
| **Purchases** (order, plan, amount, track, license code, payment reference) | Checkout | Provide and prove your license, support, accounting — *contract / legal obligation* |
| **Download history** | When you download | Enforce plan limits, provide re-downloads and license history — *contract* |
| **Technical logs** (IP, timestamp, request, browser) | Automatically | Keep the site working and secure, prevent abuse — *legitimate interest* |
| **Essential cookies** | Browsing | Keep you signed in, remember cart/preferences — *strictly necessary* |

We do **not** sell your data. We do **not** use it for third-party advertising.
[[We do not use analytics or ad-tracking cookies. If that changes, we will ask for
consent first.]]

## Payments

Payments are handled by our processors — **Stripe** (subscriptions/cards) and
**PayPal** (one-time). We do **not** see or store your full card details; the
processor does, under its own privacy policy. We receive a transaction reference,
amount, and status to record your license.

## Who we share data with (processors)

We share the minimum necessary with service providers that process data on our
behalf under contract, only to run the service:

- **Cloudflare** — hosting, database (D1), storage (R2), security.
- **Google** — "Sign in with Google" (if you choose it).
- **Stripe**, **PayPal** — payment processing.
- **Resend** — sending login and license emails.
- **YouTube Content ID administrator** (e.g. Identifyy) — only track/claim data
  needed to release Content ID claims on your licensed use; not your account data.

We may also disclose data if required by law, or to protect our rights, users, or
the service.

## Where your data is stored

Our infrastructure and some processors are in the UK, EU, US, and other countries.
Where data leaves the UK/EU, we rely on appropriate safeguards (e.g. UK IDTA /
EU Standard Contractual Clauses) offered by those providers.

## How long we keep it

- Account data: while your account is open, and for a reasonable period after.
- Purchase/license and accounting records: retained as long as needed for support
  and to meet legal/tax obligations (typically up to [[6]] years).
- Technical logs: a short period for security and diagnostics.

## Your rights (UK GDPR / GDPR)

You have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict, object to, and port your
personal data, and to withdraw consent where processing is based on consent. To
exercise any of these, email contact@tvmusicstore.com. You can also complain to
the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk.

## Cookies

We use **essential** cookies to keep you signed in and remember your cart and
preferences. These are required for the site to work. [[We do not currently set
analytics or advertising cookies; if we add any, we will show a consent banner
first.]]

## Children

The service is not directed at children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect
their data. If you believe a child has given us personal data, contact us and we
will delete it.

## Changes

We may update this policy; the version and date below change when we do, and
significant changes will be notified on the site or by email.
